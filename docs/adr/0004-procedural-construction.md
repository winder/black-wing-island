# 4. Buildings are placed by the player and procedurally generated

Date: 2026-10-03
Status: Accepted

## Decision
The player chooses what to build (e.g. a castle) and where. The building's exact shape is procedurally generated, then visibly constructs itself over about 15 seconds. Building costs Materials the player has collected.

## Rejected
- Block-by-block building like Minecraft: too slow, and castles wouldn't look like castles.
- Buildings that grow automatically from quests: takes the choice away from the player.
