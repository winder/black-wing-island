# 7. Combat (Version 2)

Date: 2026-10-03
Status: Accepted. Numbers are first guesses for Caitlin to playtest.

## Decided with Caitlin
- Attacks: **Fire Breath** (aimed where you look) and **Claw Swipe** (close up).
- Monsters fight back and hurt you. At zero health you wake up at your last village with nothing lost.
- One giant monster type per biome: Sand Snake, Giant Snail (chill), Wolf, Kraken, Yeti, Lava Worm. All are giant.
- Krakens hide in shallow water by the beaches; tentacles grab low flyers; fire makes them let go.
- Dungeon bosses come later (Version 4).

## Defaults Claude chose (change freely)
- Controls: left mouse or **E** = Fire Breath (hold); right mouse or **F** = Claw Swipe.
- Health 100; comes back on its own 5 s after the last hit. A **fire meter** drains while breathing and refills when you stop, so fire can't be used forever.
- Knocked out: "You were knocked out!" screen for a few seconds, then wake in the Home Village.
- Monsters live in fixed **dens** in their biome (the same every game). They appear within ~900 m and are put away when you leave. A cleared den fills again after 5 minutes. Monsters never enter the Home Village.
- Lava Worms barely feel fire (they live in lava), so claws are the way to beat them.
- Yetis throw snowballs at dragons flying out of reach. Wolves leap at low flyers. Sand Snakes lunge up out of the sand.
- A monster's health bar shows at the top of the screen while you fight it.
- Fights aren't saved: monsters reset when you reload.
